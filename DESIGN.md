# Trading Assistant interface contract

## Subject and job

Trading Assistant is a local operations console for one operator deciding
whether an Alpaca paper action is safe to take. Its primary job is to keep
broker evidence, safety authority, human decisions, and simulated research
visibly distinct.

The visual direction is dark-only and data-dense. Kraken's publicly documented
purple-accented density is inspiration only. The product uses an original
identity, system fonts, local assets, and no exchange trademark, copy, or
affiliation.

Green is reserved for server-verified good state. Purple identifies brand,
selection, focus, and ordinary actions. Yellow marks incomplete or cautionary
evidence. Red marks blocked or destructive state.

### Type

- Display and body: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`,
  `"Segoe UI"`, `sans-serif`.
- Data and metadata: `ui-monospace`, `"SFMono-Regular"`, `Consolas`,
  `"Liberation Mono"`, `monospace`.
- Page title: 28–34px, 700.
- Section title: 18–22px, 650.
- Metric: 24–32px, 700, tabular numbers.
- Body and controls: at least 14px.
- Label: 12px, 700, tracked uppercase only for real categorical metadata.
- Metadata: 12px, muted.
- Prices, quantities, times, percentages, and budgets use tabular numbers.

### Space and shape

- Spacing rhythm: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32px.
- Controls use a 12px radius; panels use a 14px radius.
- Ordinary controls and rows are at least 40px high.
- Consequential controls and all touch targets are at least 44px high.
- Keyboard focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(113, 50, 245, .35)`.

### State language

- **Verified**: complete, fresh evidence from the server or broker supports the
  displayed claim.
- **Caution**: evidence exists but needs operator attention.
- **Blocked**: a deterministic gate refuses the action.
- **Unknown**: evidence has not been obtained or cannot be proved.
- **Stale**: evidence is older than its declared freshness boundary.
- **Simulated**: generated by a backtest and never evidence of expected return.

Unknown and stale states never inherit verified styling. A disabled destructive
control stays legible and keeps adjacent explanatory copy.

## Layout

The shared shell is an instrument frame:

```text
┌ product / page ───── Operations · Plans · Backtests ─── operator ┐
├ ALPACA PAPER │ breaker │ daemon │ reconciliation │ observed time ┤
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Desktop `>=1180px`: navigation and proof remain horizontal; workspaces may
  use rail-and-content grids.
- Compact `760–1179px`: proof wraps and secondary rails move below priority
  decisions.
- Mobile `<760px`: one column; environment, blockers, and approval controls
  precede research.

The signature is the **proof orbit**: two offset orbital arcs around one solid
node. The node represents a claim only becoming actionable when evidence closes
the surrounding loop. It is original, local, text-free, and decorative beside
the visible Trading Assistant name.

Motion is limited to state transitions and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
Forced-colors mode preserves borders, focus, and state labels. No body-text
gradient, remote asset, remote font, trademark, or copied exchange element is
allowed.
